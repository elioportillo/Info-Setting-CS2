Info Setting CS2

Extract the exact crosshair codes and viewmodel settings of any player from any Counter-Strike 2 demo file (.dem) — including HLTV pro matches.

How to Extract CS2 Crosshair & Viewmodel from Any Demo File

Our free online tool allows you to analyze any Counter-Strike 2 replay file and instantly get the precise crosshair codes and viewmodel configurations used by the players in that match. Whether it is your own matchmaking game or a professional tournament match, you can copy their exact setup in seconds:

  1. Get your CS2 demo file: Download your own Premier/Faceit match replay, or grab a professional match demo from websites like HLTV.org.
  2. Upload the file: Drag and drop the .dem file into the box above or click browse to select it from your computer.
  3. Select any player: Once the demo analyzer processes the data, pick any player from the scoreboard to view their custom settings.
  4. Copy and paste: Instantly copy the crosshair share code or the viewmodel console commands (viewmodel_fov, offset_x, offset_y, offset_z) directly into your game.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I extract settings from professional HLTV matches?

Yes! You can extract the exact viewmodel and crosshair codes from any pro player. Just go to the "Results" or "Events" section on HLTV.org, find the match you want, download the "Demo" file, unzip it, and upload the .dem file to our tool.

How do I apply the extracted viewmodel settings in CS2?

Unlike crosshairs, viewmodels do not use a single share code. Our tool will provide you with the exact console commands (e.g., viewmodel_fov, viewmodel_offset_x, etc.). Simply copy the whole block of commands, open your CS2 developer console (~), paste them, and press Enter.

Where can I find my personal CS2 demo files?

By default, Steam downloads Counter-Strike 2 match replays to your local drive. You can find them inside your Steam directory under: \steamapps\common\Counter-Strike Global Offensive\game\csgo\replays\.

Is it safe to upload my .dem files here?

Yes, absolutely. Our tool only parses the public metadata of the match (player profiles, crosshair strings, and viewmodel variables) required to display the settings. Your game files are analyzed safely and are never stored or distributed.

Does this tool support Faceit and Matchmaking demos?

Yes, this CS2 demo analyzer supports standard Valve Matchmaking, Premier mode, and competitive third-party platforms like Faceit as long as the file format is .dem.
